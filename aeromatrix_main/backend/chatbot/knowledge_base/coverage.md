# Coverage

The trained model service supports 12 monitoring stations: Karve Road, Mhada Colony, Alandi, Bhosari, Hadapsar, Transport Nagar-Nigdi, Revenue Colony-Shivajinagar, Katraj Dairy, Savitribai Phule Pune University, Bhumkar Nagar, Panchawati Pashan and Dhankawadi. Its regions list also contains 58 wards.

Kothrud has only historical AQI, available through the AQI history route. It is not mapped to Karve Road.

Baner is a locality, not one of the 12 monitoring stations. Shivajinagar is served by the Revenue Colony-Shivajinagar station.

Not every station has a forecast for every pollutant: a station that is not represented in a model gets an UNAVAILABLE entry with the reason.
